## Directives for AI
- Jika `Definition of Ready (DoR)` == `NOT_READY`: AI DILARANG mulai koding. Lengkapi User Story, Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then), dan estimasi Story Points terlebih dahulu.
- Jika `Current State` == `WAITING_PR_MERGE` atau `SPRINT_REVIEW_GATE_4`: AI DILARANG coding task baru. Tanyakan proses PR/merge atau Sprint Review ke Product Owner.
- Jika `Current State` == `WAITING_ASSIGNMENT`: AI DILARANG membuat branch. Minta penetapan Scrum Developer dari Product Owner.
